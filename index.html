Milestone Overview

The Journey & Heritage

Key epochs celebrating Harish-Chandra's life (1923–1983) and mathematical breakthroughs.

October 11, 1923 • Kanpur

Early Life & Allahabad Education

Born in Kanpur; completed his Master's in theoretical physics at the University of Allahabad under K. S. Krishnan.

1945 – 1947 • Cambridge

Doctoral Research under Dirac

Earned his PhD at Cambridge under Paul Dirac, studying infinite irreducible representations of the Lorentz group.

1950 – 1963 • Columbia & IAS

Semisimple Lie Groups & Harmonic Analysis

Formulated character theory, discrete series representations, and harmonic analysis on semisimple Lie groups.

16 October 1983 • Princeton

Synthesis of Modern Mathematics

Cole Prize recipient (1954); unified analysis, algebra, and geometry with lasting influence on the Langlands program.
