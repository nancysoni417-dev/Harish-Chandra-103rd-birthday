Chronicles of a Genius

The Journey & Heritage

1923 – 1945 • KANPUR & ALLAHABAD

Early Education & Master's Research

Born in Kanpur; completed his Master's under physicist Sir K. S. Krishnan at the University of Allahabad, initially mastering Dirac's quantum mechanics.

1945 – 1947 • CAMBRIDGE & DIRAC

Doctorate Under Paul Dirac

Earned his PhD at Cambridge under Paul Dirac. Realized that theoretical physics lacked structural rigor and transitioned permanently to pure mathematics.

1947 – 1949 • PRINCETON & IAS

Pauli's Assistant & Turning Point

Served as assistant to Nobel laureate Wolfgang Pauli at the Institute for Advanced Study. Influenced by Hermann Weyl and Claude Chevalley, he made his definitive transition into pure mathematics and Lie groups.

1954 • AMS COLE PRIZE

Cole Prize in Algebra

Awarded the prestigious Cole Prize by the American Mathematical Society for his pioneering papers on representations of semisimple Lie algebras, cementing his stature on the world stage.

1950 – 1973 • COLUMBIA & REPRESENTATION THEORY

Semisimple Lie Groups & Harmonic Analysis

Constructed character theory for infinite-dimensional representations and harmonic analysis on reductive Lie groups, laying the analytic foundation for modern representation theory.

1973 – 1983 • ROYAL SOCIETY & FINAL LEGACY

FRS & The Bedrock of Langlands Program

Elected Fellow of the Royal Society (1973). Completed seminal work on harmonic analysis for reductive p-adic groups at IAS Princeton, creating the foundational pillars for the Langlands program.
